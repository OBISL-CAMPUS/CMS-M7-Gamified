The Integrated Marketing Communications Mix
Sort the promotional tactics into Above-The-Line (ATL) mass awareness media or Below-The-Line (BTL) targeted direct activations.
Above-The-Line (ATL): Mass media aimed at macro-level awareness (TV, Radio, National Billboards). High reach, non-targeted.
Below-The-Line (BTL): Niche, targeted activations focused on conversions (POS Displays, Pop-up stores, Direct Mail). High engagement & direct impact.

Marketing Performance Balance Scale
Adjust Ad Spend, Conversion Rate, and LTV using the balance scale controls to reach operational efficiency (LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 and ROAS ≥ 4.0).
CAC = Total Marketing Spend / New Customers
ROAS = Gross Campaign Revenue / Ad Spend
LTV:CAC = Customer Lifetime Value / CAC
Target operational efficiency ratio: LTV/CAC ≥ 3. Lower ratios indicate excessive acquisition costs.
